Before you come

How should I prepare my hair for braids?

Come with your hair washed, detangled and blown out, unless you've booked a wash with us. Clean, stretched hair means neater parts and a faster appointment.

Is braiding hair included?

Hair requirements and availability are confirmed with you when you book.

Do you take a deposit?

Yes. Braiding needs a 50% deposit to secure your appointment, with the balance paid on the day. Makeup needs a 70% deposit to confirm, with the remaining 30% paid at least seven days before your date.

What if I need to reschedule?

Please give us at least 48 hours' notice. Deposits and cancellations are subject to PHA's booking policy.

Can you come to me?

Yes. Transportation fees are charged separately and depend on the service location.

How long will my braids last?

Usually four to eight weeks. Sleep in a silk or satin scarf, oil your scalp lightly, and go easy on edge control between visits.

Can I order a custom macramé or crochet piece?

Yes. Send a photo or idea on WhatsApp with your colors and size. Production takes about 2–7 working days, depending on the design and material availability. We take a 70% deposit before we start, and the remaining 30% before collection or delivery.
